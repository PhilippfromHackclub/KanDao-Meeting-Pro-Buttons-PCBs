# KanDao-Meeting-Pro-Buttons-PCBs
These are two Buttons PCBs for the KanDao Meeting Pro in case of damage to the original ones due to a drop and afterwards detached camera module.

---
## Power & Volume Control PCB
This is the Flex PCB responsible for turning on and off the camera (left button) and controling it's volume (middle & right button).

## Mute & Mode PCB
This is the Flex PCB responsible for muting the camera (left button & LED in the middle) and changing between modes (right button).
